# Meeting notes: Maritca, Klick Kulture

## 1. Greeting (keep it light)
"Hi Maritca, thanks for making the time. I know you're busy, so I'll keep it relaxed. I'd love to tell you a bit about what I do, show you something, and mostly hear about what you guys are working on."

## 2. About me (under a minute)
"Quick background: I studied Business [Analytics] at MANCOSA. I started out on my own as a digital marketing agency for Scent Studio, doing SEO, email marketing, content and web development.

Over time I moved into what I really love: high-quality, front-end design work for companies that already have a solid reputation. Basically, making sure the way people see the business matches the quality of what the business actually delivers, whether that's through the website, the branding or the ads. I've got three clients in Joburg, including Cape Atlantic and Mokorp, and four businesses here in Knysna.

On the side I've got a startup where we build AI agents in Python: lead-qualifying agents, email sequences, and an FAQ receptionist."

**The analogy:**
"Think of two businesses offering the exact same service at the exact same price. One has a sharp website, clean ads, a brand that feels put-together. The other one looks a bit rushed. Most people choose the first one before they've even spoken to either of them, because how you look is how they assume you work. That first impression does a lot of selling before you ever get a chance to."

**Short version, if the moment's quick:**
"People judge the quality of your work by how you show up, before they ever meet you."

## 3. Let her talk (listen more than you speak)
1. "Tell me more about yourself and what you guys do at Klick Kulture. What made you want to look at this?"
2. "Where do you feel the biggest gap is right now in the quality of what you deliver to clients? Where do you think you could add more value?"
3. "How can I make your team's life easier? Where's it costing you time right now?"
4. "Where do you see videos like this fitting into what you offer clients?"
5. "Say we did work together: would this be content for SmartTechNXT only, or for your other clients too?"
6. "If this went really well, what would it look like for you in three months?"
7. "Who else needs to be happy with this on your side?"

Write down her exact words. Use them again in the quote.

## 4. Show the work (one or two videos at most)
- "Can I show you one more? I think this one shows the quality best."
- Crossroads (SmartTech) and/or the OBSIDIAN ad.
- Pause on one transition: "Every frame of that is designed. Nothing's a template."
- Then stay quiet. Let the video do the talking, and let her react first.

## 5. If she asks "How do you make these?"
"Over time I've built my own knowledge system. It's basically everything I've learned from design and past projects, written in a way that AI platforms like Claude can read and work from. So I direct it, it does the heavy lifting, and I make sure the final result is at the standard you just saw. It's still a young project, and honestly it gets sharper with every job I do."

**If she pushes ("so the AI does it?"):**
"The AI is the engine, but the system and the eye behind it are mine. Anyone can open the tools. It's knowing what good looks like that makes the difference."

## 6. Budget (ask, then go quiet)
"I've done my own research on the market, but I'd really value your take, because you've had way more hands-on experience with content at Klick Kulture. What would you normally put aside for a production video like this?"

Write the number down. Don't react to it.

## 7. Price
"To be completely upfront and transparent: a single project is R5,000. That's a full 60-second video with design, visuals, voice script and sound effects, plus two rounds of changes.

If we agree on a monthly contract of 6 to 7 videos, I can bring that down to R1,500 a video, on a minimum term so we can both plan properly.

I know you've got a strong business with a lot of opportunity where this can add value. Let me get back to you within 24 hours with a quote that works for both of us, and you can tell me any objections around that."

**If she's unsure or needs time:**
"No stress at all. Let me put it together properly and send you a quote within 24 hours, something that works for both of us and makes sure you're getting the most value out of it."

## 8. Close (always end on a next step)
- "What's the best way to send it through, email or WhatsApp?"
- "If you've got your logo and maybe two or three brands or websites you like the look of, send them over and I'll factor them in."
