## Three concepts

1. **"The Work Behind the Work"** (recommended). One glowing point in the centre of the frame. The camera dives
   through it, layer after layer: an unpaid invoice, a supplier bill, a lead gone cold, a new hire without a login.
   Each dive goes deeper into the admin, until the frame is buried. Then the point turns into an agent and flows back
   through every layer, resolving each one. Pull back to reveal a team doing growth work.
   Why: it covers the whole business, not one agent, and uses the ElevenLabs "one focal point, deeper and deeper" mechanic.
2. **"After Five"**. The business after hours. The office goes dark, but tiny points of light keep working:
   23:47, a lead answered; 02:10, bills captured; 06:00, invoices chased. The team arrives to finished work.
   Why: the sharpest emotional hook. Risk: it leans on "24/7", which every AI agency says.
3. **"Your Newest Co-worker"**. Framed as onboarding a new employee who happens to be an agent: desk, login,
   first task, first week. Why: it makes "digital co-worker" literal. Risk: cute and closer to cliché, less premium.
