name: Brand Board Collage
source: OUR approved film for Klick Kulture, "Makes you click" (2026-10-09) — klick-kulture/film/src/KK.tsx,
  klick-kulture/KlickKulture-MakesYouClick-v2.mp4. Built in one day from a one-page brand board + a services guide.
  Owner: "phenomenal… the video physically looks unique to their specific style… your skills file is working."
  Reference palette/devices = Klick Kulture (brands/klick-kulture.md); the METHOD is reusable for any brand with a
  collage/illustrated brand board. Template: templates/brand-collage-film/.
vibe_axes:
  energy: 4   # playful, a move every 1.5–2.5 s, two big hits (the snap, the roar)
  warmth: 4   # human gesture (a hand), pastel worlds, likes and hearts
  density: 3  # one hero object per beat; clutter only where the story needs noise (feed, chatter)
  realism: 3  # the client's REAL cutout photos inside a graphic world
  polish: 5
structure: the brand board is the cast. 1 grey endless feed (the problem: being scrolled past) → one post stops it →
  the brand's own gesture image (KK: a hand rising from a yellow floor) → the gesture (snap) bursts the grey into the
  brand's colours → pull back: the hero is a floating slab in a colour "playground" of the brand's collage cards →
  the brand's own headline in its own layout → a phone spins in; services land as posts, comments pop out → a cursor
  clicks; the clicks connect into a network → the network becomes "the usual checklist", ticked and crossed out →
  the brand's collage is crafted piece by piece → grey chatter buries it → the hero rises; the brand roars; the noise
  blows away → a disc swallows the frame and folds into the logo → CTA in the brand's highlight style → URL.
pacing: 40–45 s, VO-led (woman's voice, emotion-led script ~68 words); every move keyed to a word time from silence
  detection; holds only at the stop (silence) and after the snap; the rest moves continuously.
camera: 2.5D everywhere — a tilted feed plane (rotateX 24°, rotateZ −11°) with a scroll table and directional blur;
  push into one card until it IS the screen (matched-framing swap); pull back to a floating slab; lateral truck past
  depth-layered floaters; phone spins 540°→0 on Y with a real back face; 60 fps render + 2-frame blend.
transitions (owner favourites first): cursor clicks → chips → lines connect → nodes fly into check boxes ·
  grey feed rush → hard stop · card becomes the stage · colour rings from the gesture point (last ring = gradient
  world) · phone screen morphs into the next card · shockwave blows the clutter away · black disc swallows the frame
  and shrinks into the logo mark (bar + chevrons slide in, wordmark wipes out from behind).
typography: the brand's OWN fonts (KK: Aileron 800 headings, Poppins body; Archivo wide for Helios Extended); text is
  printed in the scene: typed into a post's caption, the brand's black label chips, its yellow highlight chips, its
  heading-with-side-bar layout, a form's checklist rows; one giant extended word BEHIND the subject (ROAR).
colour: the brand board used literally — off-white base, near-black ink, one hero colour as a physical surface (KK
  yellow floor), the pastels as colour rings and one full-bleed two-pastel gradient world; grey (#ECECEA) means "not
  yet seen", colour means "clicked".
ui_treatment: social-native — skeleton feed posts, an Instagram-like post (avatar = logo mark, carousel with brand
  chips, like/comment/share, caption typed in sync with the VO), white comment bubbles with coloured avatar dots,
  service chips in the brand's label style, a plain white checklist card.
motion_feel: ease-out entrances, ease-in exits, back-ease pops for stickers/chips, gravity for falling cards; still
  photos puppeted in layers (a hand rising out of a hole with a front lip; a fingertip layer that presses, flicks and
  settles for the snap); idle sway on everything held.
sound: grey A-minor feed → the pad cut dead at the stop → Dm tension → SNAP (synth snap + boom + one bell per colour
  ring) opens C major with a playful 112 bpm pulse → dry metronome for "the usual" → murmur for chatter → full C at the
  roar; plucks for every pop; ≤ 3 air moves; music −9 dB under the voice.
signature_moves:
  - one post in a grey feed types the hook line; the feed rushes; the client's gesture image stops it dead
  - the gesture (snap/click) bursts the frame into the brand's colours as concentric rings
  - the hero becomes a floating slab in a playground of the brand's own collage cards
  - the cursor's clicks connect into a network that becomes "the usual checklist", ticked, X'd and dropped
  - grey chatter buries the brand; the hero rises; one giant word behind it; the clutter blows away
  - the disc swallows everything and folds into the logo
do_not_copy: n/a (ours) — never reuse KK's hand, collages, copy or palette for another client; find THEIR board's
  gesture, surfaces and devices and rebuild each move from them. Never use photos of the client's team.
best_for: marketing/creative agencies, social-first brands, anyone with a collage/illustrated brand board, retail
  and lifestyle brands with a strong visual identity.
clashes_with: Paper Lab (too quiet), Enterprise Grid (too corporate), Signature Vivid's black base (fight over the base).
