One 5-second scroll animation. 19.2 MB of JPEG frames. 😬

We measured our own Northlight build so you don't have to guess:
• WebP frames: 8.3 MB
• WebP at half size for phones: 3.5 MB
• H.264 video: 3.0 MB, but only 2 of 150 frames are keyframes, so scrubbing stutters
• Video with a keyframe on every frame: 5.9 MB

Plus the stack we use: GSAP ScrollTrigger (now free, every plugin), Lenis for smooth scroll, and the CSS option that just landed everywhere.
