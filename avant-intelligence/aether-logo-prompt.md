# AETHER wordmark — generation prompt (matching the AVANT logo system)

Use only if you choose "Aether Intelligence". Best tools for clean lettering: Ideogram or Recraft (vector mode),
then Midjourney v7 / Google Flow (Imagen) as alternatives. Generate 8+ and pick the cleanest.

## Prompt
```
Minimal tech wordmark logo reading "AETHER" in uppercase, centered on a pure black background (#030708).
Custom geometric sans-serif letters built from straight strokes of identical thickness, wide letter spacing,
sharp angled cuts at stroke ends, no curves, no serifs.
The first letter "A" is drawn as an open chevron with NO crossbar (like the Greek lambda Λ), in neon cyan-blue
#3BC1EC, sitting slightly higher than the other letters so its peak rises above the cap height.
The letters "E", "T", "H", "E", "R" are pure white #FFFFFF: "E" made of one vertical stroke and three equal
horizontal arms, "T" with a flat top bar, "H" with straight parallel stems, "R" with a straight diagonal leg and an
angular (not round) bowl.
Flat vector, 2D, perfectly crisp edges, no gradient, no glow, no shadow, no 3D, no texture, no tagline, no icon,
no extra text. Brand identity for an AI company. Aspect ratio 2:1.
```

## Negative prompt (where supported)
`curves, rounded letters, serif, script, gradient, glow, bevel, 3D, shadow, texture, extra letters, misspelling,
crossbar on A, icon, symbol, mockup, background pattern, watermark`

## Sub-line (add later in vector, not in the generator)
"INTELLIGENCE" in a light geometric sans (e.g. Manrope 300, OFL), tracking ~0.6 em, centred under the wordmark,
width ≈ 55 % of the wordmark.

## Honest note
Image generators often break lettering (wrong letters, uneven strokes). The exact match is to build AETHER as a real
vector from the traced AVANT glyphs: reuse the blue Λ and the T as they are, and draw E, H, R with the same stroke
weight and end cuts. That's about 30 minutes of work and gives you a file you can trademark and print.
