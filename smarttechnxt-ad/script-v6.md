## Motion direction (either option)

The camera never sits still. It moves in all directions, and every cut is motivated by movement.

| Beat | Camera / transition | Elements | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| Documents named | Documents DROP from above one per word; camera tilts DOWN with each, snap-cut on impact | ID card, utility bill, bank statement pages with realistic layouts | Paper whoosh + soft thud per doc |
| Opened / checked / retyped | Whip-pan RIGHT to screen 1, RIGHT to screen 2, DOWN to screen 3 (the same number typed three times) | Three system UIs, blinking cursor, typed digits | Key clicks, whip swoosh on each pan |
| One wrong digit | Hard PUSH-IN to a single digit turning red; everything else blurs | Mismatch highlight, "Rejected" stamp | Low error tone + stamp thud |
| File bounces back | The file physically flies BACK up out of frame (reverse of the drop) | Rubber-band motion | Reverse whoosh |
| Client waits. Days. | Camera pulls UP and away; a calendar flips fast underneath | Day tiles flipping, clock sweep | Clock ticks accelerating, then cut to silence |
| Some don't wait | A client card slides OUT left and the seat stays empty | Empty profile slot | Single soft "blip", silence |
| Product in | Lime line SLICES the frame diagonally (wipe transition), and the robot dot drops in from the top | Lime node, the logo "T" motif | Riser into a clean hit |
| Read / verify / open | Fast vertical scroll DOWN through the documents while the scan runs; ticks fire on the beat; camera SPINS 90° into the account card | Scan line, data chips flying into fields, green ticks, "Account open" | UI clicks, tick pings on each word |
| Logged / audit-ready | Camera DOLLIES along an audit trail that builds right to left | Timestamped log rows | Soft typing ticks |
| Exceptions / rest | Split screen: a person on one side (one flagged file), robots on the other (a stream of files) | Split wipe from the centre | Two-tone sting |
| Logo | Everything collapses into the lime line and it draws the "T", then the lockup | Logo, tagline, CTA | Logo sting + low boom, music out |

Music: a modern minimal pulse of about 110 to 120 bpm (not piano) that drops out at "One wrong digit" and hits back at the product reveal.
