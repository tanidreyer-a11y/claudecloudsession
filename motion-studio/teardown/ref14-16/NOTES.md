## What to take (and what not)
Take the motion grammar: persistent UI anchor while worlds change behind it, cursor + click as the cut, morphs
between objects (trail → bar, button → light rings → whip), push through frames (windows, pills), letters assembling,
subject in front of headline, full-frame heroes with soft bloom dissolves, rolling word lists. Never their logos,
copy, people or exact UI; and for premium studios don't sell "AI builds your site in seconds" (the prompt-bar
message) — keep the device, change what's in it.
