name: Deep Glass
source: ref03.mov — "NeuraFlow AI" product teaser by Zelios (vertical recording of a 16:9 film). Studied for style only.
vibe_axes:
  energy: 3   # confident, smooth, a little showy
  warmth: 2   # cool blue, softened by glow
  density: 3  # a full dashboard appears, then the camera isolates pieces
  realism: 3  # a believable product UI, rendered as frosted glass
  polish: 5   # glass, bloom, star-dust, depth
structure: hook (full dashboard "Hello, Mark — how can I help?" flies past, 0–8 %) → feature word (giant "New" behind a glass ring and a floating icon button, 8–30 %) → interaction (prompt bar slides across, "AI Agent" answer card builds, 30–70 %) → feature carousel (3D curved row of glass cards with icons rotating past, 70–100 %)
pacing: 9.3 s teaser, 2 hard cuts (avg shot 3.1 s); continuous camera within each shot; music-led ~118 bpm.
camera: strong push-ins and lateral slides at close range (the UI is bigger than the frame); a curved carousel orbit at the end; shallow depth of field so only one panel is sharp.
transitions: fly-through UI (camera passes through the dashboard into one element) · scale-through glass ring · horizontal slide/parallax of stacked panels · 3D carousel rotate · hard cut on the downbeat.
typography: clean grotesk (Inter/SF-like) for UI; one giant extra-bold display word at 300–400 px half-hidden behind glass; white text with blue-tinted shadow.
colour: deep navy base #0A1224 / #152447, royal blue panels #1E3A71 / #295399, highlight #3C78C9 → ice #7BA9DE edges and glow; star-dust specks; no warm colours at all.
ui_treatment: frosted-glass panels (backdrop blur, inner highlight, 1 px light edge), circular glass icon buttons, gradient selection bars; the product is real-looking but re-skinned in glass.
motion_feel: smooth ease-out slides with slight parallax between layers; glass edges catch a moving specular light; no bounce.
sound: modern electronic, punchy downbeats, risers into each cut, sparkle SFX on glints; loud (−13.8 LUFS).
signature_moves:
  - a giant single word sitting behind a glass ring with a floating icon button
  - the camera skims across an oversized glass UI with only one row in focus
  - curved 3D carousel of glass feature cards
  - star-dust field behind everything
do_not_copy: the NeuraFlow name/UI, the dashboard layout, the "New" composition verbatim, Zelios' music.
best_for: AI assistants, dashboards, launch teasers, feature announcements, investor/demo-day sizzles; 9:16 and 16:9.
clashes_with: Paper Lab, Lavender Desk, Cream Cascade (light families), Desert Glass's warmth, anything handmade or rustic.
