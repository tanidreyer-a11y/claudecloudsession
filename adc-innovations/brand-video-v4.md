| VO | Ball / line |

| Step into a world... | It lands in light; the camera pushes through into a clean new space. |
| Almost. | Freeze. |
| Meet ADC agents... each one precise | The ball splits into agents, each snapping onto its own line. |
| Read / check / act | Three precise ticks along the line. |
| Nothing slips through your fingers. | The same drop moment as before, but the ball holds the line. |

| Precision... at work. | The ring resolves into the logo. |
