name: Crossroads
source: OUR approved film: SmartTech NXT "Crossroads" (smarttechnxt-ad/, examples/smarttech-crossroads.*). The founder
  "loved it". House technique, colour-neutral here: SmartTech's navy/lime/Museo Sans belong to brands/smarttechnxt.md only.
vibe_axes:
  energy: 3   # brisk: a new beat every 1.5–2.5 s, carried by one continuous camera
  warmth: 2   # precise and technical, with one warm human beat (robots alongside people)
  density: 3  # one idea per beat, but rich: systems, documents, messages, records
  realism: 2  # pure geometry + stylised UI; no photography
  polish: 5
structure: logo anatomy as plot. 1 crossroads (a line rises, meets a junction, splits) → 2 the problem in four beats
  (systems alone and links breaking · data typed twice with an error · paperwork stacking while the camera tilts up ·
  messages multiplying faster) → 3 "signal lost in the noise" → 4 THE TURN: the bridge is built from the logo's own
  parts → 5 proof (robots alongside people · connects existing systems · reads a document · moves every detail where
  it belongs) → 6 trust (every step recorded, every decision traceable) → 7 "right on target" (x marks the spot +
  the dot) → 8 signal clearer / picture sharper → 9 the logo assembles from the parts the film used → tagline → URL.
pacing: 57 s VO-led (Oliver, ~105 words); every motion keyed to a word time (W); hook word with held silence; a
  beat every 1.5–2.5 s; zero dead frames; 1–2 hidden cuts inside zoom-throughs.
camera: monotone spline through word-relative keys, log-space zoom, ±3–6° roll in orbits, tilt-up with stacking
  paper, dive into the target dot (zoom-through) and pull back to reveal the next scene; 60→30 fps motion blur.
transitions: zoom-through iris (hidden cut) · object becomes object (chips → ball → logo dot; a flat line → the
  logo's bar) · line-led (a line draws, branches, camera follows the tip) · rack focus on "sharper" · noise → clean
  signal crossfade through a vivid midpoint.
typography: the brand's own font, left-aligned (bible rule); small mono labels on UI; the only big type is the
  wordmark + tagline sized to the wordmark width.
colour: brand-locked — use the client's tokens: dark brand base + texture + faint precision grid + ONE accent ≤ 5 %
  (thin lines, dots, glow), white type. Problem act cooler and noisier, light lifts at the turn.
ui_treatment: geometry first (lines, nodes, rings, crosshairs), then stylised UI: system tiles, a document read by a
  beam, message bubbles, an audit log, robot/agent glyphs next to people glyphs. No device frames, no photos.
motion_feel: soft springs on elements, eased OUT entrances (bezier 0.16,1,0.3,1), faster exits, 3–6 frame staggers,
  small breathing on holds; messages accelerate (stagger shrinks) to feel like overload.
sound: ambient electronic bed, chord change at the turn, UI sounds as chord notes, ≤ 3 air moves, a held silence on
  the hook word, −16 LUFS.
signature_moves:
  - the logo's parts cast as the story's props (the bridge, the antenna, x marks the spot, the dot as the target)
  - a single line that rises to a crossroads and splits
  - "signal lost in noise" → clean signal as the problem/solution hinge
  - every object turns into the next one, ending as the logo
do_not_copy: SmartTech NXT's logo, colours, font and tagline outside SmartTech films; reuse the TECHNIQUE, rebuild
  the anatomy from each new client's own logo.
best_for: B2B software, automation, AI agents, fintech/infra, any brand whose logo has parts with meaning; brand films
  and website heroes, 45–60 s, VO-led.
clashes_with: Kinetic Pop, Sunset Prompt, Cream Cascade, Desert Glass (photo- and warmth-led styles).
