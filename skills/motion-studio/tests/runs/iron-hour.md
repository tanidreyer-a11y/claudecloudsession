### Recipe A — Cream Cascade base × Cobalt Campaign donor
_Parts drawn from: Cream Cascade, Cobalt Campaign, Midnight Signal. Wildcard slot: hook. Needs: imagery._

- **structure**: Craft reveal — raw material → process → finished product → people enjoying it
- **pacing**: Short punch — 10–20 s, 0.4 cuts/s, sparse sound
- **camera**: Vertical travel — falls, drops and rises following one object
- **transitions**: Duplicate-and-offset cascade + Line draws and branches; camera follows the tip + Freeze and slow to 5 % on the hook word
- **typography**: Editorial serif headlines, wide tracking caps labels
- **colour**: Cream + espresso + peach + terracotta
- **ui_treatment**: Photographic creatives on cards (ads, products, listings, dishes)
- **motion_feel**: Soft drops that settle into stacks, 100–150 ms staggers
- **sound**: Warm cinematic bed, light percussion, very few SFX
- **hook**: Sensory macro shot (crust cracking, perfume mist, chalk dust)
- **fonts**: Playfair Display (OFL), Cormorant (OFL), Bodoni Moda (OFL)

### Recipe B — Kinetic Pop base × Sunset Prompt donor
_Parts drawn from: Kinetic Pop, Sunset Prompt, Deep Glass. Wildcard slot: ui_treatment. Needs: nothing extra._

- **structure**: Statement → statement → proof wall → CTA (music-led)
- **pacing**: Upbeat — beat every 1–1.5 s, cuts on the downbeat
- **camera**: Locked frame — motion lives in type and elements
- **transitions**: Hard cut on the downbeat + Vertical word roll / slot-machine swap + Colour-block step wipe
- **typography**: Heavy rounded statements filling the width
- **colour**: Paper ↔ charcoal flips + one loud gradient for hero frames
- **ui_treatment**: Nodes and gradient tiles joined by elbow lines
- **motion_feel**: Snappy springs with overshoot, 40–80 ms staggers
- **sound**: Punchy electronic with risers into downbeats
- **hook**: Headline with one rolling word slot
- **fonts**: Poppins (OFL), Nunito (OFL)
