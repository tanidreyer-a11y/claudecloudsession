# Obsidian brand bible v1.0 (30 October 2026)
Pages: `pages/01.jpg` to `pages/09.jpg` (1920 x 1080). Logo files: `logo/`. Colour tokens: `colours/obsidian-tokens.css`. Fonts: `fonts/` (SIL OFL).

**Brand.** Obsidian is a creative studio for the web: front-end design and UI/UX, scroll-scrub animated websites, 3D interactive websites.
Tagline: "Digital precision that builds reputation." Voice: plain, confident, premium; never pitch on price; never template work.

**Logo.** The lockup is the emblem (two parallel bars rising at 34.9°) plus the OBSIDIAN wordmark, a custom geometric logotype (never type it).
White on black or the dark plate; black on light. Emblem alone for avatars, favicons, watermarks, cover corners.
Clear space: half the emblem's width on every side. Minimum: emblem 24 px (6 mm); lockup 160 px (40 mm) wide.
Don't: purple or gradients (the violet version was retired on 10 Oct 2026), other colours, stretching, rotating, glows, busy grounds.
Files: `obsidian-lockup-white.png` / `-black.png` (transparent; wordmark from the official lockup, emblem from its vector geometry),
`obsidian-emblem-white.svg` / `-black.svg` (vector).

**Colour.** Black #000000 (dominant) · White #F4F5F7 · Electric blue #2F6BFF (light, not paint) · Deep blue #0A1330 ·
Lightbulb white #FFFFFF with a #FFE7C2 halo. Glow system: plate #0A0A0C, silver #A9ADB6, body #C4C6CD, muted #8E919B, ink #0B0C0E.
Any other hue only where the content needs it, one per scene. No purple.

**Type.** Archivo for everything: display 800 at width 125 %, titles 650 at 112 % in sentence case with a white-to-silver gradient,
body 400. JetBrains Mono for code only. Never Inter. No ALL-CAPS tracked labels.
