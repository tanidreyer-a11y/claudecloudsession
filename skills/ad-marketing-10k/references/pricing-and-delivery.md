# Pricing & delivery

## Market ranges (South Africa, 2026, 60 s animated/motion graphics)
| Tier | Price |
|---|---|
| Entry / freelancer | R5,000 – R20,000 |
| Mid-range studio | R30,000 – R80,000 |
| High-end studio | R80,000 – R250,000+ |
| Realistic minimum for professional B2B motion graphics | R20,000 – R25,000 |
Sources: astralstudios.co.za, audiovisuallab.co.za (studio-published, lean high). International freelance 60 s
explainer ≈ $1,000–5,000.

## Strategy
- **Anchor at value, discount visibly** for a first/founding client ("normally R25k, founding-client R18–20k").
  Never let the discounted number become the price.
- Push a **retainer** (consistency beats a one-off): Starter 2 short videos/month R10–12k; Growth 1 mid + 3 short R18–22k.
- Add-ons: cutdown 15–30 s incl. 9:16 R3–4k; logo sting R2–3k; extra revision R1,500.
- Know your cost: hours × rate + ElevenLabs + music licensing + tax.

## Terms
50 % deposit / 50 % on delivery · 2 revision rounds · 7–10 working days from approved script & voice ·
client owns the final video; project files not included · retainer minimum term + 30 days' notice.

## Meeting script
Listen first. Ask: where will the video live; how many per month/quarter; next campaign; who approves; is there a
budget. If pressed for a number: give the anchored range, then stop talking. Follow up with a written quote.

## Platform note (state the metric)
Short video wins reach on algorithmic feeds (Reels/TikTok). On LinkedIn B2B, video has the lowest median CTR
(~0.24 %) but cheap reach; static ads often win cost-per-lead. Use the film for reach/retargeting and trust;
a strong still or carousel to harvest clicks.
