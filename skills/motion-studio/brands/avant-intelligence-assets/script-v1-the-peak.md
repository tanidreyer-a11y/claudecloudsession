# Avant Intelligence: "The Peak" (brand film, ~60 s)

Same flow as SmartTech NXT "Crossroads": pain → the brand arrives → what it does → logo anatomy → name → line → CTA.
The story comes from the logo:
- **Avant** = "ahead", "forward" (French: *before*). You stay ahead of the work.
- The blue **Λ** = an A with no crossbar: two lines rising to one peak. Your people on one side, your agents on the
  other, meeting at one point: your decision.
- **No crossbar** = nothing standing in between.

Voice: Oliver (Multilingual v2, Speed ~0.87, Stability ~30, Similarity ~80, Style ~48). Paste exactly, including the
blank lines.

```
Every business... is climbing.

But the work... piles up at the bottom.

Invoices. Reminders. Documents.

Leads... waiting for a reply.

And the higher you climb... the heavier it gets.

Avant Intelligence... lifts the weight.

AI agents... working alongside your people.

Each one... trained for a single job.

They read every document.

Chase every invoice.

Answer every lead... even after hours.

And every decision... comes to one point.

You.

You approve.

It's done.

Two lines... rising to one peak.

Your people. Your agents.

Nothing... in between.

Avant Intelligence.

Ahead... of the work.

Book your demo today.
```

~105 words, about 58–62 s at Oliver's pace.

## Claims check
- "AI agents", "trained for a single job", documents/invoices/leads: matches what Avant offers. Confirm the four
  agent roles.
- **"even after hours"**: only if the lead agent really replies outside office hours. Otherwise change it to
  "Answer every lead... in seconds", and only if that is true.
- "Ahead... of the work." is a suggested tagline, not trademarked. Check it before using it in print.
- No statistics. "Twenty tasks automated" from the old ADC script is gone on purpose (unverified).
- CTA: swap in the website once the domain is registered ("Visit avant intelligence dot co dot za").
