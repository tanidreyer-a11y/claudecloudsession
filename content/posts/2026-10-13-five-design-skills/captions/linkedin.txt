AI-built websites have a look. You've seen it: Inter, a purple gradient, the same rounded cards.

Anthropic explained why: without direction, a model samples from the "high-probability center" of its training data. The fix isn't a better prompt every time. It's giving Claude a point of view before it writes code.

These are the 5 skills I'd install first as a web designer:

1. frontend-design (Anthropic): plans the palette, type and layout, then checks the plan against generic defaults
2. Impeccable (Paul Bakaus): 24 commands for audit and polish, plus 59 rules that detect AI design tells
3. UI/UX Pro Max: generates a design system from your brief
4. GSAP skills (GreenSock): correct ScrollTrigger, pinning and scrub for scroll-driven sites
5. webapp-testing (Anthropic): Claude screenshots its own build in a real browser
