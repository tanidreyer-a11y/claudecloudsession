5. DESIGN SYSTEM: "OBSIDIAN GLOW" (owner-approved 2026-10-20)
- Canvas: carousel 1080 x 1350 (4:5). Reel and story 1080 x 1920 (9:16). Stills as JPG quality 95. Video as H.264 MP4. Never PDF. Never send a grid or contact sheet as a deliverable.
- Type: Archivo (Google Fonts) only. Titles weight 650, width 112 percent, tracking -0.03em, sentence case. Body weight 400, 36 to 42 px. JetBrains Mono for code only. Never Inter. No ALL-CAPS tracked labels, no "WORD - fragment" labels, no emoji on slides.
- Colour: off-black #0A0A0C. White #F4F5F7. Body grey #C4C6CD. Muted #8E919B. Dark ink #0B0C0E (the only text colour allowed on the bright glow). Electric blue #2F6BFF only as a single accent or chart highlight. No purple of our own (purple may appear inside third-party screenshots).
- THE PLATE (our own background): off-black with one soft white glow rising from the bottom edge, plus fine grain. Recipe: radial-gradient(112% 50% at X 104%, #FFFFFF 0%, rgba(255,255,255,.97) 30%, rgba(228,232,235,.74) 50%, rgba(160,168,176,.30) 70%, transparent 90%) over #0A0A0C. X is 22% (bottom left), 50% (centre) or 78% (bottom right); change it from slide to slide. Add a faint white sheen (about 13 percent) from the opposite top corner. Add film grain (SVG noise, overlay, about 30 percent). Neutral white only, no hue.
- Plate over footage: when a video or screenshot sits behind the glow as texture, blur it 14 px, brightness 45 percent, saturation 75 percent, so only colour and movement remain.
- Brand marks: the Obsidian emblem (two parallel slanted bars rising to the right, white) plus the word "Obsidian", top left of covers. Identity block on covers and call-to-action slides: round photo, "Nathaniel Dreyer", "Building premium digital experiences.", Instagram glyph and @_studioobsidian. Add the X glyph and handle only after the owner supplies it. The owner supplies the portrait and the emblem files.

6. WHAT GOES ON THE SLIDE (choose one mode per post)
A. UI SNIPPET: a 4 second screen recording of the real UI (cropped, no browser bars, no notches), in a rounded container on the plate. Preferred for showcase, proof and tool posts.
B. SCREENSHOT: one still of the UI, same container. Use it when motion adds nothing.
C. PLAIN PLATE: text on the plate, no screenshot. Default for news, saves, checklists and ideas.
D. CLAUDE MARKS: only on posts about Claude. The Claude symbol sits on the cover above the title. Clawd (the Claude Code robot) may appear small on about half the slides, never covering text, never recoloured or redrawn. The last slide then carries: "Claude, Claude Code and Clawd are trademarks of Anthropic. Obsidian is not affiliated with Anthropic." Never use these marks on a post that is not about Claude. Anthropic has not approved our use; the owner chose it.
Rule of choice: a post about a thing with a UI uses A or B. A post about an idea uses C. A post about Claude uses C or A, plus D.
Third-party footage: use it only as blurred texture, or as a clearly credited screenshot of a public page (name and domain on the slide). Crop out other people's logos and footers when it is used as texture. "We built" appears only on sites the owner confirms are ours.

8. REEL RECIPE (1080 x 1920, 60 fps, silent unless told)

Each clip gets its own background: near-black with a soft glow in that site's own dominant colour, and a partner colour from the top corner.

11. TRUTH RULES (non-negotiable)
- Every factual claim traces to a primary source opened that day: the official blog, changelog, repo or docs page. Listicles, X threads and YouTube summaries are leads only.

- Never invent people, quotes, handles or results. Never guess a handle. Never present someone else's work as ours.
