# Image prompts — client hero sections (these sit INSIDE browser windows, never full frame)

Tool: Google Flow (Imagen) or Higgsfield; also Midjourney v7 / Adobe Firefly. 16:9, ≥ 2560×1440, 4 variations each.
Same look in every prompt: *dark, cinematic, low-key lighting, deep blacks, a single magenta-violet rim light,
shallow depth of field, premium editorial, no text, no logos, no people's faces in focus*.
Save with these exact names; send them back and I drop them straight in.

```
HERO 1 — hero_guesthouse.jpg
Prompt: Boutique guesthouse suite at dusk, linen bed and stone wall, warm lamp inside, a soft magenta-violet glow
  through the window, cinematic low-key light, deep blacks, 35mm, shallow depth of field, premium editorial,
  space on the left for a headline, no text, no logos.
Negative: text, watermark, people, cluttered room, bright daylight, HDR, oversaturated.

HERO 2 — hero_practice.jpg
Prompt: Quiet professional office at night, black walnut desk, a single brass lamp, leather chair, city lights
  blurred behind the glass, a thin magenta-violet edge light on the desk, cinematic, deep blacks, 50mm, shallow
  depth of field, space on the right for a headline, no text, no logos.

HERO 3 — hero_venue.jpg
Prompt: Empty events venue before guests arrive, long table set with glassware, candlelight, haze in the air,
  magenta-violet uplighting on the back wall, cinematic, deep blacks, wide 24mm, no text, no people.

HERO 4 — hero_product.jpg
Prompt: A premium matte-black wireless speaker floating in a dark studio, exploding into thin layered parts
  (exploded view), magenta-violet rim light, black background, product photography, ultra sharp, no text, no logo.

HERO 5 (optional) — hero_restaurant.jpg
Prompt: Fine-dining plate on black slate, smoke curling, a single violet back-light, macro food photography,
  deep blacks, no text.
```
Each one ends up inside a browser window with a headline, nav bar and button that I build in code, e.g.
"Stay somewhere that remembers you." That's why they need empty space on one side.
