name: Kinetic Pop
source: ref05.mp4 — a "SaaS Product Demo" template-style promo (16:9). Studied for style only.
vibe_axes:
  energy: 5   # hype; something changes every beat
  warmth: 4   # friendly, human faces and avatars, rounded type
  density: 3  # bold single statements, but many elements per scene in the proof section
  realism: 2  # graphic type + stylised cards/avatars, one photo
  polish: 3   # clean and confident, template-grade rather than luxury
structure: hook (word-swap headline "Your ✓ Product / App / Agency / Story", 0–14 %) → promise ("Show what matters" + image strip, 14–35 %) → proof (big number + rising arrow, testimonial wall, "Trusted by thousands" avatars, metric cards, 35–80 %) → CTA ("Let's make it happen" + gradient "Book a demo" button + cursor click, 80–100 %)
pacing: 22.95 s, 7 hard cuts (avg shot 2.9 s, 0.31 cuts/s) PLUS many in-shot changes; ~1.5 audio onsets/s on a ~118 bpm beat. Fastest in the proof section, slows only for the CTA click.
camera: mostly static frame; motion comes from type and elements. Occasional push on the number/arrow.
transitions: hard cut on the beat, slot-machine/vertical word roll, colour-field swaps (white ↔ charcoal ↔ gradient), elements flying in from off-frame, a cursor click as the final beat.
typography: heavy rounded geometric sans (Poppins-like, 700–800), huge statements filling the width; brand-blue highlight words; kinetic behaviour: vertical word rolls, word-by-word reveals, words split around an inserted object (coin, photo strip).
colour: paper white #FCFAFD (44 %) and charcoal #2B282A (34 %) alternate per scene; one loud blue→violet→red gradient (#3F68DC, #9AA3CE, #AE7174) for hero frames and the CTA button; no grain.
ui_treatment: floating metric cards (tilted, rounded, drop shadow, green +% chips), testimonial cards with stars, avatar bubbles, a checkbox icon; no device frames.
motion_feel: snappy springs with a little overshoot (pop), fast 40–80 ms staggers, elastic scale-ins, quick ease-out entrances.
sound: upbeat electronic/pop track ~118 bpm, cuts land on beats, light whoosh/pop SFX on entrances; no VO (text-led); loud (−14.2 LUFS).
signature_moves:
  - headline with one rolling word slot ("Your ✓ Product → App → Agency → Story")
  - an object dropped between two words (coin between "Convert" and "faster")
  - big number with a hand-drawn-feel rising arrow
  - tilted metric cards raining in with green growth chips
  - gradient pill CTA clicked by a cursor
do_not_copy: the template's exact layouts and copy ("Show what matters", "Let's make it happen"), the people photos, avatar artwork, the music; never invent the metrics or testimonials it shows.
best_for: Reels/TikTok/Shorts ads, launches for SMB SaaS, apps, agencies, e-commerce tools, events; anything music-led without a voiceover.
clashes_with: Midnight Signal and other calm/luxury styles; long VO-led stories; legal/financial brands that need gravitas.
