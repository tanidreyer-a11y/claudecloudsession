and the neon line stay visible around them at all times.

## D. Feel (recipe — engine base: Cobalt Campaign/NIVO, forced by request; colour overridden by brand)
- Structure: pain (flat template site) → the turn (it shatters, OBSIDIAN arrives) → 3 build loops (type a business
  → its cinematic hero assembles on a tilted plane) → proof (desktop + phone, scroll-scrubbed) → brand → WhatsApp.
- Focus point (one object, whole film): a **neon cursor/caret** that types the brief, cuts the old site, draws each
  hero, and finally becomes the dot in the end card.
- Camera: tilted 3D planes (15–25°), push to the prompt, pull back to grids of heroes (from Cobalt Campaign).
- Transitions: send-is-the-cut · giant glowing word the camera flies through ("Reputation") · tilt-away/tilt-in ·
  shatter (their monolith footage as the hidden cut).
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque, huge light-weight words with a pink→purple glow (NIVO's chapter words, our colours).
- UI: glass URL/prompt bar, browser windows, phone frames, status pills (LIVE · SCROLL-SCRUBBED · PHONE-TUNED).
- Motion: smooth ease-out, layered parallax, motion blur. Sound: cinematic half-time pulse, a swell per hero.
- Length/format ✱: 16:9 · 50–60 s (website + LinkedIn) + 9:16 cut-down 25–30 s (Reels/WhatsApp).
