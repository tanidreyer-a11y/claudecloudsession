## How the "52 blog system" actually works
- `content_calendar` table: 52 weekly rows (topic, target_keyword, phase: foundation / authority /
  conversion / compounding), admin-only. Rows are NOT seeded in migrations; they exist only in the DB.
- `/admin/calendar` -> "Draft" creates a `blog_posts` draft and opens the editor.
- `generate-blog-draft` edge function writes 900-1200 word posts via Gemini 2.5 Flash (Lovable AI gateway).
- `/blog` and `/blog/:slug` read published posts from Supabase. Post page has canonical, OG and BlogPosting JSON-LD.
- `sitemap` edge function builds XML from static routes + published posts.

## Findings (ranked by impact)
| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical | Sitemap is never exposed: no `/sitemap.xml`, not in robots.txt, and vercel.json rewrites every path to index.html, so `/sitemap.xml` serves the app shell. | Add a vercel rewrite `/sitemap.xml` -> sitemap function URL (or build-time sitemap), add `Sitemap:` line to robots.txt, submit in Search Console. |
| 2 | Critical | Client-side rendered SPA. All meta tags (Helmet) and blog content appear only after JS runs. Google can render it (slowly); WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram link previews cannot. | Prerender (e.g. vite-plugin-prerender / static build of /blog/* and /perfume/*), or move to SSR. Biggest single lever. |
| 3 | High | Product, catalog, home, about, contact, reviews, quiz pages set NO per-page title/description/canonical. Every product page shares the homepage title. | Add Helmet per page; Product JSON-LD (name, price in ZAR, availability) on /perfume/:id. |
| 4 | High | Sitemap omits all `/perfume/:id` product pages. | Generate product URLs into the sitemap. |
| 5 | High | 3 hand-written posts in `src/data/blog-posts.ts` are dead code; nothing renders them. Unless they were also entered into Supabase, they are not live. | Confirm in /admin; if missing, import them as published posts. |
| 6 | Medium | Site-wide og:image and LocalBusiness image point to the Lovable placeholder image. | Branded 1200x630 OG image. |
| 7 | Medium | LocalBusiness opening hours list Tuesday only. Wrong hours data can show in Google. | Correct full week hours; type `Store`; match Google Business Profile exactly. |
| 8 | Medium | Homepage meta description is generic ("world's most exquisite fragrances") with no SA / inspired / oil-based keywords. | Rewrite (draft below). |
| 9 | Low | AI drafts are all Gemini Flash, 900-1200 words, same structure. Risk of thin, templated content. | Human edit pass each week; add first-hand detail (store, real customer questions, wear tests). |
