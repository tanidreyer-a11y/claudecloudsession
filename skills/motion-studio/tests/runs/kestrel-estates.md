### Recipe A — Lavender Desk base × Desert Glass donor
_Parts drawn from: Lavender Desk, Desert Glass, Cobalt Campaign. Wildcard slot: motion_feel. Needs: imagery._

- **structure**: Verb spine (Analyze · Research · Review · Deliver) — one chapter per verb
- **pacing**: Measured — new beat every 1.5–2 s, in-camera scene changes
- **camera**: Locked frame — motion lives in type and elements
- **transitions**: Background wash changes colour/family between chapters + Tilt away / tilt in of planes + Light-matched photographic cross-dissolve
- **typography**: Serif display (gradient) + clean grotesk UI
- **colour**: Lavender / mint / cream washes per chapter + one violet accent
- **ui_treatment**: No UI — product, place and people only (non-software businesses)
- **motion_feel**: Soft, high damping, long ease-in-out — nothing snaps
- **sound**: Light pulse ~120–130 bpm, clicky UI ticks, no big hits
- **hook**: The place through a lens
- **fonts**: Fraunces (OFL), Newsreader (OFL), Inter (OFL)

### Recipe B — Desert Glass base × Paper Lab donor
_Parts drawn from: Desert Glass, Paper Lab, Midnight Signal. Wildcard slot: ui_treatment. Needs: imagery._

- **structure**: A day in the life — morning problem → midday turn → evening calm
- **pacing**: Cinematic — new beat every 2–3 s, 0–2 hard cuts
- **camera**: Tilted 3D planes (15–25° Y), push to prompt, pull back to grids
- **transitions**: Glass lens / rack-focus reveal + Light-matched photographic cross-dissolve + Tilt away / tilt in of planes
- **typography**: Editorial serif headlines, wide tracking caps labels
- **colour**: Golden hour → dusk: rose, sand, coral, rust, dusk blue
- **ui_treatment**: Frosted glass panels, inner highlight, 1 px light edge
- **motion_feel**: Soft, high damping, long ease-in-out — nothing snaps
- **sound**: Warm cinematic bed, light percussion, very few SFX
- **hook**: The place through a lens
- **fonts**: Playfair Display (OFL), Cormorant (OFL), Bodoni Moda (OFL)

### Recipe C — Cream Cascade base × Desert Glass donor
_Parts drawn from: Cream Cascade, Desert Glass, Midnight Signal, Cobalt Campaign. Wildcard slot: motion_feel. Needs: imagery._

- **structure**: Craft reveal — raw material → process → finished product → people enjoying it
- **pacing**: One take — zero hard cuts, one continuous camera journey
- **camera**: Vertical travel — falls, drops and rises following one object
- **transitions**: Line draws and branches; camera follows the tip + UI strips away into the logo + Card flip reveal
- **typography**: Editorial serif headlines, wide tracking caps labels
- **colour**: Cream + espresso + peach + terracotta
- **ui_treatment**: Nodes and gradient tiles joined by elbow lines
- **motion_feel**: Smooth ease-out with layered parallax
- **sound**: Warm cinematic bed, light percussion, very few SFX
- **hook**: Before/after split or slider
- **fonts**: Playfair Display (OFL), Cormorant (OFL), Bodoni Moda (OFL)
