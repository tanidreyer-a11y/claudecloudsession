# Plotter Ink (reference look — "Motion as Code", pdoom-video by mexicat, MIT, vendor/pdoom-video)
Use only when the owner asks for this energy. Obsidian Precision stays the default.
- Palette: ink #0A0A0B, raised ink #151517, graphite #5E5B57, ash #9C978F, bone #EEE9DF, one signal orange #FF4D12
  (ember #FF8A3D for cores, blood #C21D0B for shadows). One rare accent per film at most.
- Type: Archivo across widths (animate width), Cormorant serif (lining figures), IBM Plex Mono. → `LOOKS.plotter`.
- Plates: each scene a pure function of time, placed by content anchors (`lineOf`, `wordOf`), cuts 0.18 s early.
- Finish: bloom + halation, chromatic aberration at the edges, tone shoulder, grain 0.04–0.1, vignette;
  4–12 sub-frame motion blur (shutter 0.5) on fast moves.
- Sound: hundreds of small cues from word timings, ducked up to 7 dB under the voice; −14 LUFS.
- do_not_copy: their song, lyrics, scenes or characters (removed from the vendored copy). Engine ideas only.
