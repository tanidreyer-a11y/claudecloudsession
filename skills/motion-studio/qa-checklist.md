# QA checklist — run on the ENCODED web file, not the master

Extract ~20 frames (every beat plus the middle of every transition), view them as a contact sheet, and actually look.
Tick every line or fix and re-render. Paste the filled list into the delivery message.

## Story & truth
- [ ] Hook lands in the first 2 s (something moves in the first 15 frames).
- [ ] One clear problem/desire, one CTA. The CTA is readable for ≥ 2 s.
- [ ] Every number, claim, certification, price and name is verified (brand profile) or flagged to the client.
- [ ] No invented testimonials, clients, results or reviews. Unreleased features are not shown as live.
- [ ] Demo data is fictional and identical everywhere; no real personal data; "-minded", not "compliant", unless verified.
- [ ] Spelling/region (en-ZA vs en-US), currency and phone/address formats are correct.

## Recipe fidelity & originality
- [ ] Matches the chosen recipe (list any deviation and why).
- [ ] No reference assets copied: logos, footage, music, characters, taglines, exact layouts (each card's do_not_copy).
- [ ] Not a repeat: shares ≤ half its slots with the previous project; signature move differs from this brand's last film.
- [ ] Another client's palette, fonts or signature (ADC Innovations, SmartTechNXT) was not used unless this IS that client.

## Picture
- [ ] No empty frames; no stop-start camera; no element visible before its entrance or after its exit.
- [ ] Text: effective size (px × zoom) ≥ 24 px at 1080p when read; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (≥ 3:1 at 27 px+);
      nothing clipped or overlapping; title-safe; 9:16 critical content in the middle 75 %.
- [ ] One focal point per frame; accent ≤ ~5 % of pixels; the hero colour on one element at a time.
- [ ] Logo is the extracted vector, correct colours and clear space; never redrawn or distorted.
- [ ] Images: no AI artefacts (hands, faces, text, labels), consistent light and grade across the set, rights recorded.
- [ ] Motion inventory printed; no treatment repeated across content types.
- [ ] No banding in dark gradients (grain on), BT.709 tags present.

## Sound
- [ ] Every hit lands on its word or syllable (±0.1 s); hook pauses where intended.
- [ ] ≤ 3 different air or whoosh moves; UI sounds are notes in the current chord; music ducked under VO.
- [ ] Integrated loudness −16 LUFS (or −14 for short-form if agreed), true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP (measure it).
- [ ] Music and SFX sources are royalty-free, self-generated or licensed, and listed in the README.

## Files
- [ ] Master (CRF 18) + web/share copy under its size limit (chat 30 MB, WhatsApp 15 MB) for every format asked.
- [ ] Duration matches the brief and the VO; no dead air at the end.
- [ ] Project README: recipe, sources (fonts, music, images + prompts/tools), claims list, versions.
- [ ] Recipe recorded in history/projects.json; feedback logged after the client responds.
