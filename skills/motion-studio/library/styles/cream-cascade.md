name: Cream Cascade
source: ref04.mov — ElevenLabs "localized ads" vertical clip (an ad-production workflow shown as a diagram). Studied for style only.
vibe_axes:
  energy: 3   # steady flow, lots of things arriving
  warmth: 4   # cream paper, peach/coral tones, beauty-product imagery
  density: 4  # many ad variants stacking down the frame
  realism: 4  # real photographic ad creatives inside the diagram
  polish: 4
structure: source (play/pause tile on cream, 0–15 %) → branch (connector lines grow to channel tiles "Meta / Google / LinkedIn Ads", 15–35 %) → multiply (one ad becomes a cascade of localized variants: EN, ES, JA, AR, FR…, 35–85 %) → control (gradient orbs "Voice", "Tone" float in as dials, 85–100 %)
pacing: 10.2 s, ZERO cuts; continuous downward scroll/camera; ~0.8 onsets/s, slow ~78 bpm feel.
camera: slow vertical scroll down the cascade, slight lateral re-frame; things enter from below and stack.
transitions: connector lines draw from node to node · duplicate-and-offset (a card clones and slides into the stack) · scroll-reveal · orbs fade/scale in as the last beat.
typography: elegant serif on the ad creatives (product brand), tiny grotesk labels on tiles; no big headline — the creatives are the content.
colour: cream #F4F0ED (48 %), espresso #443834, peach #DDB293, terracotta #A9826F; channel tiles in soft multi-colour gradients (violet→green, coral→pink).
ui_treatment: rounded nodes and gradient tiles connected by thin elbow lines; ad cards with light grey frames and drop shadows; flags as tiny dots beside each variant.
motion_feel: soft ease-out drops, 100–150 ms staggers on stacking cards, lines draw with ease-in-out.
sound: gentle downtempo bed, soft clicks as each variant lands; ≈ −14.4 LUFS.
signature_moves:
  - one creative multiplying into a cascade of variants (languages, sizes, audiences)
  - flowchart elbows connecting a source tile to channel tiles
  - gradient orbs as "controls" (Voice, Tone)
do_not_copy: the "AVELOA" creatives, the model photography, the ElevenLabs layout.
best_for: ad agencies, marketing tools, localisation/translation, e-commerce catalogues, franchise/multi-branch businesses, any "one input → many outputs" story. NEEDS IMAGERY (see imagery.md).
clashes_with: Deep Glass, Midnight Signal (dark), Kinetic Pop's charcoal flips.
