# Crumb & Co — image prompt sheet (example of imagery.md step 3)

Recipe A (Desert Glass × Cream Cascade): day-in-the-life · parallax over photography · golden hour → dusk · editorial
serif · frosted glass order panel. The client sends real photos of the shop and products first. These prompts fill the gaps.
Tool: Google Flow (Imagen) or Higgsfield; also Midjourney / Adobe Firefly. 4 variations each. No text in any image.
Shared look words (in every prompt): *warm natural window light, shallow depth of field, 35–85mm, cream and terracotta
palette, editorial food photography, Johannesburg morning, film-like grain, no text, no logos*.

```
SHOT 01 — street_dawn.jpg  · 9:16 · ≥1440×2560 · used 0:00–0:03 (place through a lens, slow dolly in)
Prompt: Quiet tree-lined suburban high street in Johannesburg at dawn, jacaranda trees, a small bakery shopfront with
  warm light glowing inside, empty pavement, soft golden haze, cinematic, 35mm, shallow depth of field, no text, no signage.
Negative: text, signage, logos, people's faces, cars in focus, oversaturated, HDR look.

SHOT 02 — dough_hands.jpg · 4:5 · ≥1600×2000 · used 0:03–0:07 (macro, Ken Burns 1→1.08)
Prompt: Close-up of floured hands folding sourdough on a worn wooden bench, flour dust in a beam of early window light,
  cream and terracotta tones, 85mm f/2, editorial food photography, no text.
Negative: text, watermark, extra fingers, jewellery, plastic look, blur on hands.

SHOT 03 — oven_reveal.mp4 (video, Higgsfield / Flow Veo) · 9:16 · 4 s · 24 fps · used 0:07–0:11
Prompt: Deck oven door opening, steam rolling out, golden loaves on a peel slide toward camera, slow push in, warm light,
  no cuts, no text.

SHOT 04 — croissant_break.jpg · 4:5 · used 0:11–0:14 (sensory macro)
Prompt: Macro of a butter croissant torn open, flaky layers, steam, crumbs on a dark walnut board, warm side light, no text.

SHOT 05 — counter_evening.jpg · 9:16 · used 0:24–0:30 (dusk close, glass order panel floats over it)
Prompt: Bakery counter at dusk, last loaves on cooling racks, warm pendant lights, blue hour outside the window, calm,
  editorial, 35mm, no text, no people.
```
Save the files with these names in `public/img/`. Until they arrive, `<Photo>` renders "IMAGE PENDING" placeholders at
the right size and timing. Record the tool and prompt per image in the project README.
