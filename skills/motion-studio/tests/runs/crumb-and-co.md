### Recipe A — Desert Glass base × Cream Cascade donor
_Parts drawn from: Desert Glass, Cream Cascade, Cobalt Campaign. Wildcard slot: motion_feel. Needs: imagery._

- **structure**: A day in the life — morning problem → midday turn → evening calm
- **pacing**: Measured — new beat every 1.5–2 s, in-camera scene changes
- **camera**: Slow dolly + parallax over a photographic backdrop
- **transitions**: Tilt away / tilt in of planes + Light-matched photographic cross-dissolve + Glass lens / rack-focus reveal
- **typography**: Editorial serif headlines, wide tracking caps labels
- **colour**: Golden hour → dusk: rose, sand, coral, rust, dusk blue
- **ui_treatment**: Frosted glass panels, inner highlight, 1 px light edge
- **motion_feel**: Soft drops that settle into stacks, 100–150 ms staggers
- **sound**: Warm cinematic bed, light percussion, very few SFX
- **hook**: The place through a lens
- **fonts**: Playfair Display (OFL), Cormorant (OFL), Bodoni Moda (OFL)

### Recipe B — Cream Cascade base × Lavender Desk donor
_Parts drawn from: Cream Cascade, Lavender Desk, Midnight Signal, Paper Lab. Wildcard slot: ui_treatment. Needs: imagery._

- **sound**: Warm cinematic bed, light percussion, very few SFX

- **fonts**: Fraunces (OFL), Newsreader (OFL), Inter (OFL)

### Recipe C — Cobalt Campaign base × Desert Glass donor
_Parts drawn from: Cobalt Campaign, Desert Glass, Cream Cascade. Wildcard slot: camera. Needs: imagery._

- **structure**: Craft reveal — raw material → process → finished product → people enjoying it
- **pacing**: Measured — new beat every 1.5–2 s, in-camera scene changes
- **camera**: Macro detail → wide reveal (photographic)
- **transitions**: Tilt away / tilt in of planes + Camera passes through a giant word + Background wash changes colour/family between chapters
- **typography**: Huge light-weight gradient chapter words
- **colour**: Cobalt/indigo darks alternating with ice-blue lights; photos keep their warmth
- **ui_treatment**: Photographic creatives on cards (ads, products, listings, dishes)
- **motion_feel**: Soft, high damping, long ease-in-out — nothing snaps
- **sound**: Cinematic half-time pulse (~75/150 bpm) with a swell per result
- **hook**: Before/after split or slider
- **fonts**: Montserrat (OFL), Manrope (OFL)
