# Story & concept

## The structure that won (45–60 s)

| Act | Share | Job | Example lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Pain | 0–30 % | Name the hidden work with concrete artefacts; end on loss | "Every business runs on work... that nobody sees." / "And every day... something slips." |
| 2 Turn | 30–40 % | The promise, then a twist that sets up control | "Step into a world where the work does itself." / "Almost." |
| 3 Proof | 40–75 % | One real case, step by step, precise | "They read every document. Check every detail. Act... in seconds." |
| 4 Trust | 75–88 % | Human in control, auditability, callback to Act 1 | "Nothing slips through your fingers. Nothing leaves without you." / "One loop." |
| 5 Brand | 88–100 % | Name → tagline → one CTA | "ADC Innovations. Precision, at work. Book your fifteen-minute demo." |

## Concept engines

### A. Logo-anatomy story (best result)
Read the brand bible's logo explanation and cast each logo part as a prop:
- SmartTech NXT: double **T** = crossroads + bridge → open on a T-junction, "builds the bridge" = the logo's
  antenna bar flying in to crown the double T; **xx** = target crosshair; **dot** (pétanque cochonnet) = the ball that
  rolls to dead centre on "right on target"; antenna = "signal clearer, picture sharper" (noisy wave → clean wave).
- The film ends with the logo **assembling from those same parts**, so the reveal feels inevitable.

### B. One focal point, deeper and deeper (ElevenLabs principle)
A single dot/orb/line stays near frame centre. Scenes are found *inside* the previous one (zoom-through).
Viewer's eye never searches → feels premium and calm even at high pace.

### C. Same journey twice
Act 1: the path gets stuck, turns red, "bounces back" (camera rockets backward). Act 2: the same dive, flowing.

### D. Callback motif
Plant a physical event early and reverse it late: ball **slips** off a line → ball **holds** the line on
"nothing slips". Freeze on "Almost." → resolve on "Meet the agents".

## Niche selection
Offer two niches with reasoning (who buys, how painful, how visual). Example from a client:
- A) Client onboarding / KYC (FICA in South Africa) for financial services — high stakes, compliance.
- B) Employee onboarding for HR — relatable, smaller budgets.
Explain local jargon (e.g. FICA = Financial Intelligence Centre Act, SA anti-money-laundering KYC law).
