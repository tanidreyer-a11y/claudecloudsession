# Focus Thread (reference look: one-shot focus-point reel the owner loves, 2026-10-15)
This card is for the **method**. Colour stays Obsidian Precision unless the owner asks otherwise.
- **Idea:** one white point leads the eye for the whole film. It becomes every element (nodes, ring, star, icon,
  pill, card), and a thread carries it, and the camera, from one UI pill to the next.
- **World:** black void with fine dust. A tone inversion to a white world mid-film, then back to black for the logo.
- **Type:** small, light UI labels of 1–3 words ("Build structure", "Create rhythm"). Here: Archivo 400, about 48 px
  on 1080 wide.
- **Motion:**
  - the camera follows with lag and banks into curves;
  - it pulls out mid-travel and pushes in to enter;
  - shape morphs instead of cuts, and contact floods;
  - blur-to-sharp text, with motion blur only on fast camera moves.
- **Transitions:** morph, thread ride, track matte, camera pull-back, and one match cut. Method in
  `.claude/skills/obsidian-video-studio/continuity.md`; kit `src/fx/Camera.tsx`; proof `ONESHOT`.
- **Their colour (not ours):** blue/cyan/magenta gradient glows. Ours: white light sweeps on graphite. Never purple.
- **do_not_copy:** their words, layouts, pill sequence or the M logo. We take the method and build our own frames.
