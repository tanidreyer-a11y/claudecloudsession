# Script: feel, pace and voice

The wording is usually fine; **the feel and pace** is what needs craft. This file is about rhythm.

## 1. The ElevenLabs rhythm
- Talk to get the point across → **pause to let it sink in** → repeat. Pauses come at the *end of a thought*,
  not after every phrase.
- Each paragraph = one idea, 4–12 words. A blank line between paragraphs is the breath.
- Inside a line, use "..." **only** to put weight on the second half: `And every day... something slips.`
- One-word paragraphs are punches: `Almost.` `One loop.` `Precision.` Use 1–3 per film.
- Rule of three for momentum: `They read every document. / Check every detail. / Act... in seconds.`
- Parallel pairs for authority: `Every step... recorded. / Every decision... traceable.`
- Callbacks: reuse a key word from Act 1 in Act 4 (`slips` → `Nothing slips through your fingers`).

## 2. Tempo maps
| Mode | Words / 60 s | Read speed | Pauses | Music | Visual beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Premium calm (default) | 95–110 | ~0.85–0.9 | 0.4–0.7 s between paragraphs; 1.5–2 s around the hook | pad + soft 60–120 bpm pulse | every 2–3 s |
| Upbeat / Reels | 120–140 | ~1.0–1.1 | 0.2–0.4 s; no long holds | 110–128 bpm, kick on downbeats | every 1–1.5 s, cut on beats, kinetic type |
| Short cutdown 15–20 s | 35–50 | 0.95–1.05 | one hook pause | hit at logo | hook in first 2 s, logo by 15 s |

Formula: `seconds ≈ words / 1.8 + 0.5 × paragraphs + hook pauses` (calm). Check before sending.

## 3. Proven voice settings
- **ElevenLabs Multilingual v2, "Oliver Silk – Deep Gravel Narrative"**: Speed 0.87, Stability 30, Similarity 80,
  Style 48. Client-approved; deep, slow, elegant, not whispery. Paste text with blank lines.
- **Eleven v3/v4 with audio tags** (`[softly] [warmly] [pause]`) gives more emotion but tended to **whisper**; offer as
  an option, not the default. Tags are read aloud on v2 — never mix.
- `<break time="1.0s" />` works on v2/Turbo/Flash (max 3 s); too many cause artefacts.
- Generate 3–4 takes, pick the calmest; regenerate single lines only if needed (keeps tone consistent).

## 4. Delivering a script
Always: a code block ready to paste (blank lines included) + settings + a claims check
(verified / to confirm) + expected duration. Offer the cut order if it may run long.
