# ADC Innovations v6: modelled on the dark Eleven Agents ad's story flow

Their flow (from the frames): countless requests fall in → many channels branch to one line → one agent (the orb)
→ a real, specific conversation → connected knowledge → always on (24/7) → name + one-line descriptor + CTA.
The same flow, in our words below. The voice is the narrator's words and is not modelled (music masks it in the recording).
