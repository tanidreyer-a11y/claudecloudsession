Claude can animate now.

@claudeai Motion launched in beta on 8 Oct. You describe the animation, Claude writes code that animates your text, charts and images, and you download an MP4. No video model, so no generated footage and no AI people.

The catch: Team and Enterprise only, for now.
On Pro or Max? HyperFrames (open source, by HeyGen) does the same job inside Claude Code. Install commands on slide 6.

Also on 8 Oct: Claude Design, Docs and Slides came out of beta, on every plan including Free.

Save this for your next build.
