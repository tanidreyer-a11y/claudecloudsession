# OBSIDIAN brand film — "Digital precision that builds reputation" · voice script v1 (~50–53 s · 105 words)

Voice: conversational premium male modelled on the ElevenLabs ads (ref13: mid-low ≈120 Hz, ≈135–150 wpm, close, dry,
near-continuous with short pauses). Tone: quiet confidence, warm, unhurried but never slow. Not salesy, no hype.

```
You've spent years building a reputation.

The work. The clients. The standard.

But online... you look like everyone else.

Same template. Same scroll. Same silence.

At Obsidian, we don't start with a template.

We start with code.

Every line... precise. Every pixel... intentional.

Branding with an edge.

Websites that move the way your brand does.

Ads that make people stop... and remember.

And then... we make it work.

AI agents, powered by Avant Intelligence.

They answer. They qualify. They book.

At two in the afternoon... or two in the morning.

Your presence doesn't just stand there anymore.

It works.

Obsidian.

Digital precision... that builds reputation.
```

## ElevenLabs settings (start here, test first)
- Model: Multilingual v2 · Speed 1.0 · Stability 45 · Similarity 80 · Style 20 · Speaker boost on. No break tags.
- Voices to test (default library, conversational male): Eric, Chris, Brian — or Voice Library search
  "conversational narrator male calm". Generate the FIRST FOUR LINES with 2–3 voices and send the takes: they get
  measured against ref13 (pitch, pace, pauses) before the full take.
- Pace is fixed by words, not the slider: if it runs long, cut words.
