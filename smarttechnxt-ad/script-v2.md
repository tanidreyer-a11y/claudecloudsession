# SmartTechNXT: "The Quiet Weight" (v2 voiceover script)

Length: ~90 s at a slow, premium female read (~1.8 words/sec, ~165 words).
ElevenLabs: Stability 45-55, Similarity 75, Style 15-25, Speed 0.88-0.92.
Line breaks = breath. "..." = held pause. Render in ONE take, not per line.

Brand (from logo): Navy #163456 (ground), Lime #B2CB07 (light / hero accent), White #FFFFFF (type).

Sources used for claims (smarttechnxt.com via search): finance/AP automation with audit trail and
shorter close cycles; HR onboarding, time tracking, benefits admin; customer onboarding
(document processing, validation, account setup); "selecting tools for ROI, integrating legacy
systems, bridging business silos"; "smart technology in service of people". No statistics invented.
