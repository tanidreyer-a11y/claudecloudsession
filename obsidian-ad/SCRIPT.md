# OBSIDIAN — "Show the work" (VO ~55 s, ElevenLabs)

Voice ✱: a warm, confident narrator (Oliver works; a slightly younger voice suits a design studio better: try
"Brian" or "Daniel" too). Multilingual v2 · Speed ~0.9 · Stability ~35 · Similarity ~80 · Style ~40.

```
Right now...

your business doesn't quite show... the quality of the work you do.

Same template. Same stock photos.

And a buyer decides... in seconds.

Your website... is the meeting you don't attend.

So we build it... to speak for you.

A guesthouse... that feels like the stay.

A practice... with the weight of its name.

A product... that turns, opens and assembles... as you scroll.

Every frame designed.

Every scroll... a story.

Built for the phone in your client's hand.

OBSIDIAN.

Websites... with the weight of your reputation.

Send us a message on WhatsApp.
```
~95 words. Lines 5 and 14 come from your own site copy ("the meeting you don't attend", "carry the weight").

Alternative opening (your second line): "Build a flawless reputation... with designs that look the part."
I'd keep "Right now... your business doesn't quite show the quality of the work you do": it names the pain
the viewer already feels, and the hook lands in the first three seconds.
