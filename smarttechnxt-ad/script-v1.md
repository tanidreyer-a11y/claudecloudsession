ElevenLabs tips: Stability ~45–55, Style ~15–25, Speed ~0.9. Line breaks = breaths; "..." = held pause.

## Plain script (paste into ElevenLabs)

Every new client... begins with paper.

Forms. Copies. Signatures.

Checked... re-checked... filed.

Hours your team will never get back.

What if onboarding... simply flowed?

SmartTechNXT deploys intelligent software robots —
that read your documents, validate every detail,
and set up each account... precisely.

No stacks. No bottlenecks. No errors waiting to be found.

Just your people... free to do the work that matters.

SmartTechNXT.
Smart technology... in service of people.
