name: Paper Lab
source: ref02.mov — ElevenLabs light product film (16:9 web/hero). Studied for style only.
vibe_axes:
  energy: 2   # calm, scientific; one thing moves at a time
  warmth: 3   # soft organic gradient orbs warm up an otherwise clinical page
  density: 2  # one card or one diagram per moment, huge white space
  realism: 2  # real-looking UI cards and charts, but floating on paper, no screenshots
  polish: 5   # meticulous: hairlines, perfect alignment, tiny type done right
structure: hook (a metric line graph drops from 4.8 to 4.1 — the problem in one curve, 0–10 %) → diagnose (alert card "High severity", line branches into Variant A / Variant B orbs, 10–35 %) → control (guardrail toggles flip on one by one, 35–55 %) → trust (certification badge grid, lock-in-cloud, wireframe globe in a soft green fog, 55–85 %) → resolve (two orbs merge, curve recovers, loop back to the first graph, 85–100 %)
pacing: 31.3 s, ZERO hard cuts (measured). ~1.5 audio onsets/s on a light ~130 bpm pulse. A new element every ~2.5 s; long sections are one continuous camera move. Ends where it began (loop).
camera: slow lateral drifts following a hairline, gentle push-in onto a card, rotate-free; depth via soft blur on background fog only.
transitions: line-led (a hairline draws and branches, camera follows its tip) · morph (orb → globe → lock) · match on shape (circle outlines become orbs) · fog wash (background blooms from paper to green gradient and back) · no cuts.
typography: neutral grotesk (Inter-like) at small sizes, regular/medium; numerals in brand blue/red for the data points; labels in tiny caps; nothing above ~48 px — the diagrams are the headline.
colour: paper #F6F3F4 (76 %) + cool grey #D8DDE2; sage/teal fog #ABBBB6 / #7B9A96 / deep #415A4A; data accents periwinkle and a single red for "bad"; iridescent gradient orbs (blue/green/yellow) as the hero objects. No grain.
ui_treatment: floating white cards with 1 px borders and soft shadow; pill toggles; line icons for certifications; charts drawn as thin vector lines with an area fill at 15 % opacity.
motion_feel: long ease-in-out, zero overshoot; hairlines draw at constant speed then ease out; toggles snap with a tiny 120 ms settle; orbs drift and slowly rotate their internal gradient.
sound: soft clicky UI ticks (toggles), airy pad, light pulse ~130 bpm, no big hits; integrated ≈ −19.3 LUFS (recording) → master to −16.
signature_moves:
  - a KPI curve that dips and is later repaired (problem and payoff in one graphic)
  - one hairline that branches into A/B variants ending in living gradient orbs
  - guardrail toggles flipping on in a stagger
  - certification badge grid drawn in line icons
  - film loops back to its opening frame
do_not_copy: ElevenLabs' gradient-orb artwork as-is, their guardrail copy, the exact badge set (show only certifications the client truly holds).
best_for: AI/ML platforms, analytics, compliance/security, health-tech, fintech infra, any product whose value is "control and evidence"; website heroes and LinkedIn.
clashes_with: Kinetic Pop, Sunset Prompt (gradients fight), heavy photography, loud music-led edits.
